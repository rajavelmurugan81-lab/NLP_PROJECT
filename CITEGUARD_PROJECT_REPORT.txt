CORE RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION: CLOSED-LOOP FAILURE-PATTERN MEMORY
================================================================================
CiteGuard implements the complete closed-loop architecture:

USER RESEARCH QUESTION
  ↓
PLANNER AGENT
  ↓
PAPER RETRIEVAL (OpenAlex + Local BM25/FAISS/RRF)
  ↓
CLAIM EXTRACTION (Semantic Entity Decomposition)
  ↓
CLAIM + EXACT EVIDENCE (Passage Extraction & Mapping)
  ↓
CLAIM-EVIDENCE VERIFICATION
  ├─► [PASS] ──► MEMORY GATE ──► TRUSTED MEMORY (trusted_memory.json)
  │
  └─► [FAIL] ──► FAILURE CLASSIFIER
                  ↓
                FAILURE SIGNATURE
                  ↓
                HALLUCINATION LEDGER (hallucination_ledger.jsonl)
                  ↓
                HISTORICAL FAILURE MATCHING (failure_patterns.json)
                  ↓
                SIMILARITY / RISK ASSESSMENT (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, VERY_HIGH)
                  ↓
                ADAPTIVE VERIFICATION (Depth, Scope, Contradiction, Debate Escalation)
                  ↓
                CLAIM RE-CHECK (ACCEPT / REVISE / REJECT)
                  ↓
                FINAL ANSWER

1. FAILURE PATTERN EXTRACTION & MINING
================================================================================
CiteGuard separates specific Failure Signatures (individual observed error instances)
from generalized, reusable Failure Patterns derived from benchmark and runtime ledgers.

Stored in: results/hallucination_ledger/failure_patterns.json

All 9 Canonical Patterns:
1. FP_DOM_001: OFF_DOMAIN_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTED_AS_SUPPORT (DOMAIN_MISMATCH)
2. FP_IRR_002: WEAK_OVERLAP_PASSAGE_INCLUDED (IRRELEVANT_EVIDENCE)
3. FP_RET_003: GROUND_TRUTH_EVIDENCE_MISSED_IN_TOP_K (RETRIEVAL_FAILURE)
4. FP_UNG_004: SUPPORT_VERDICT_WITH_SUBTHRESHOLD_EVIDENCE (UNGROUNDED_SUPPORT)
5. FP_CNF_005: HIGH_CONFIDENCE_ON_SPARSE_EVIDENCE (UNGROUNDED_CONFIDENCE)
6. FP_CON_006: UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE_OVERLOOKED (CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE)
7. FP_NEI_007: FALSE_SUPPORT_ATTEMPT_ON_EMPTY_EVIDENCE (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE)
8. FP_MET_008: UNVERIFIED_BIBLIOGRAPHIC_METADATA (METADATA_MISMATCH)
9. FP_CLM_009: AWKWARD_GERUND_OR_NONSENSICAL_CLAIM (CLAIM_MALFORMATION)


================================================================================
2. HISTORICAL FAILURE MATCHING & RISK ASSESSMENT
================================================================================
When a claim and candidate evidence are evaluated:
1. FailureMemory checks structural conditions against all 9 learned patterns.
2. Quantifies a Similarity/Risk Score (0.0 to 1.0) and assigns a Risk Level:
   - LOW: Standard multi-agent verification.
   - MEDIUM: Expanded passage relevance filtering, floor enforcement.
   - HIGH: Strict domain topic validation, sufficiency verification.
   - VERY_HIGH: Debate escalation, cross-checking, and Claim Re-Check.


================================================================================
3. ADAPTIVE VERIFICATION & CLAIM RE-CHECK
================================================================================
Matching a failure pattern does NOT trigger an automatic or unilateral failure rejection.
Instead, it triggers Claim Re-Check under heightened adaptive scrutiny:
- Checks if candidate passages belong to an excluded or off-domain topic.
- Disqualifies sub-threshold passages (< 0.50 relevance floor).
- Checks for unaddressed contradiction signals.
- Dynamically recalculates confidence (capped at <= 0.35 on missing or off-domain evidence).

Resolves into one of three canonical actions:
- ACCEPT: Evidence withstands deeper scrutiny; verdict confirmed.
- REVISE: Claim qualified or downgraded from SUPPORT to PARTIAL/CONTRADICTED.
- REJECT: Spurious off-domain or ungrounded evidence rejected; verdict revised
  from false SUPPORT to INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
